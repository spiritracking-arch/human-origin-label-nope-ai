# Human Origin Creator Charter

Version 1.0 · Published at [human-origin-label.lochness-paris.com/charter/](https://human-origin-label.lochness-paris.com/charter/) · CC BY 4.0


**Version 1.0.** This charter sets out what every creator displaying a Human Origin badge commits to, and what we commit to in return. It applies to both badges: “Self-declared” and “Process reviewed”.

## 1. The principle

The work covered by your badge is made by you. You may use tools, including AI tools, for supporting tasks. You may not use AI to generate or substantially rewrite the text, images, sound or footage that make up the work.

## 2. What is allowed and what is not

Everywhere: research, references and inspiration are allowed, including from AI tools, as long as you create the work itself. Presenting AI-generated parts as your own is never allowed.

| Discipline | Allowed | Not allowed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Writing | Spelling and grammar checkers that correct errors; dictionaries and thesauruses; research tools | AI-drafted text; AI rewriting of sentences or paragraphs; AI-generated outlines used as the structure of the work; AI translation presented as your writing |
| Illustration | Digital brushes, filters and standard software tools; references and photo studies | Generated images, including as a base to paint over; generative fill or expand; AI style transfer |
| Photography | In-camera processing; exposure, color and crop adjustments; noise reduction and sharpening; removing dust or small distractions | Generative fill or expand; generated skies, objects or people; composites using generated elements |
| Music | Instruments and plug-ins; mixing and mastering tools, including AI-assisted ones; stem separation of your own recordings | AI-generated melodies, harmonies, beats or lyrics; synthetic or cloned voices; generated samples |
| Video | Editing, color grading, stabilization; automatic captions of your own speech | Generated footage or animation; synthetic voices or avatars; face or voice swaps; AI-written scripts |

In a gray area? Ask us before you publish: [contact@lochness-paris.com](mailto:contact@lochness-paris.com). When in doubt, the work is not covered.

## 3. Your commitments

- **Honesty.** Everything you show us during a review is genuine and relates to the work covered by your badge. A screen recording must be made for this review and show the one-time code we give you.
- **Your own website only.** Display your badge on your own website, profiles or publications. A reviewed badge belongs to the creator named on its verification page and cannot be transferred.
- **Only for covered work.** Place the badge next to the work it covers, or in a general spot (footer, about page) only if all the work you publish there follows this charter.
- **Watermarks on covered work only.** Reviewed creators may add the Human Origin watermark to images and videos covered by their review. It must stay legible: do not crop, blur or cover the badge number.
- **Your accounts, listed.** Display your badge or watermark only on the website and social accounts listed on your verification page, and tell us when they change.
- **Unaltered badge.** Use the code we provide as is: do not edit the image, remove the link or change where it points.
- **No overstatement.** Do not describe the badge as a certification, a guarantee or an official label. Say that your creative process was reviewed, or that you declare following the Human Origin standard.
- **Keep us informed.** If your practice changes and your work no longer follows this charter, remove the badge and let us know.

## 3b. Certified works and fingerprints

Reviewed creators can have individual works certified. For each one, the creator computes the digital fingerprint (SHA-256) of the original file on our Check a file page, in their own browser, and sends us only that code. It is published on their verification page with the title and date, so that anyone can check that a file is exactly the certified work. A fingerprint must only be submitted for a work covered by the review, and for its final version.

## 4. Our commitments

- **A fair review.** We look at your evidence with care and explain our decision, including when we decline.
- **Your files are never kept.** Reviews take place live on a video call where you share your screen, or from a screen recording you send through an encrypted app or an expiring link. We delete any recording within 7 days of our decision and keep only a short note of what we saw.
- **Transparency.** Your verification page states what was reviewed, when and how. It never claims more than that.
- **Your data.** We handle it as described in our [privacy policy](https://human-origin-label.lochness-paris.com/privacy/).

## 5. Revocation

We may revoke a badge if it is displayed in breach of this charter, in particular if the work covered includes AI-generated content, if evidence shown during the review was not genuine, or if the badge is used on someone else’s work.

Self-declared badges are not tied to a named creator, so for them revocation applies to the website where the badge is displayed: every Human Origin badge on that site then shows as revoked.

Except in cases of obvious fraud, we first contact you by email and give you 14 days to explain or correct the situation. If we revoke the badge, its verification page states so publicly, the badge shows “Badge revoked” on every site that displays it, and you must remove it.

## 6. Leaving the program

You can leave at any time by writing to us. We delete your verification page and your badge stops working. Please remove it from your website.

## 7. Changes to this charter

Each version of this charter is numbered. We inform reviewed creators by email at least 30 days before a new version applies. If you do not accept it, you can leave the program as described above.

## 8. How you accept this charter

- **Self-declared badge:** by adding the badge to your website.
- **Process-reviewed badge:** by confirming in writing, before your verification page is published, with this sentence: *“I have read and accept the Human Origin Creator Charter, version 1.0, and I agree that my verification page is published.”*
