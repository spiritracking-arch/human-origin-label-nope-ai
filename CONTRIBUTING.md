# Contributing

Human Origin is meant to become a shared standard, so its rules are discussed in the open.

## How to propose a change

1. **Open an issue** describing the problem and the change you suggest. Say which section of the
   specification or the Charter it affects.
2. For wording fixes, a **pull request** against `spec/` or `charter/` is welcome.
3. The editor answers every issue. Accepted changes go into the next version and the
   [CHANGELOG](CHANGELOG.md).

## How changes are decided

- During the pilot, the editor decides, and explains each decision in the issue.
- A change to the meaning of a status, an identifier or a review requirement is a MAJOR version.
- Changes to the Creator Charter are announced to reviewed creators at least 30 days before they apply.
- Each release is archived on Zenodo with its own DOI.

## What this repository is not

It does not contain the Human Origin service itself (registry, review tools, admin). Creator
records and review notes are never published here.

Please be respectful: critique rules, not people.
